Community Standards

Roleplay with respect.

The website keeps the essentials easy to understand. Your full rules and enforcement details should remain available in Discord.

01

Respect the Community

Treat players and staff respectfully. Harassment and toxic behavior have no place in the community.

02

Roleplay Properly

Avoid random deathmatch, vehicle deathmatch, metagaming, and other behavior that breaks roleplay standards.

03

Play Legit

Cheats, exploits, macros, and unauthorized advantages that affect gameplay are prohibited.

04

Protect the Economy

Do not abuse jobs, bugs, or systems for illegitimate money, rewards, items, or progression.
